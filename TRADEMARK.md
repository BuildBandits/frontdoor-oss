# Trademark Policy

This document describes how the **Frontdoor™** name, logo, and related marks may be used. It complements — and does not replace — the project's source-code license.

## Status

"Frontdoor" is an **unregistered trademark** claimed by the project's original authors under common-law rights established by the public use of the name in connection with this software. The `™` symbol is used to assert this claim.

The maintainers may pursue formal registration in one or more jurisdictions in the future. This document will be updated if and when that happens.

The MIT license (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)) covers the **source code**. It does **not** grant any rights to use the Frontdoor name, logo, or brand identity.

## Why a trademark policy

The MIT license lets anyone fork, modify, and redistribute the code. We support that — it is the point of being open source. The trademark policy exists to keep one specific guarantee in place: **users looking for "Frontdoor" should land on the official project, not a re-branded fork that looks the same but is operated by a different team.**

## What is allowed (fair use)

You may use the name "Frontdoor" without asking us in these cases:

- **Referring to the project.** Blog posts, articles, talks, comparisons, tutorials, documentation that mention Frontdoor by name.
- **Stating compatibility.** "Works with Frontdoor", "Frontdoor integration", "Frontdoor plugin for X", "Migrated from Frontdoor" — as long as the statement is accurate.
- **Unmodified redistribution.** Packaging or hosting an unmodified copy of Frontdoor for internal use, including under its original name. (The MIT license already permits this; we are confirming it.)
- **Forks that clearly identify themselves.** A fork that keeps the name "Frontdoor" in its history and attribution, but distributes itself under a clearly different product name and identity.

## What is not allowed

You may **not**, without prior written permission:

- Distribute a modified version of Frontdoor under the name "Frontdoor" (or a confusingly similar name) in a way that suggests it is the official project or endorsed by the maintainers.
- Operate a commercial hosting, SaaS, or managed-service offering under the name "Frontdoor" or a confusingly similar name.
- Use the Frontdoor logo or visual identity in your own product, organization, or marketing material in a way that implies affiliation or endorsement.
- Register domain names, social media handles, package names, or company names that incorporate "Frontdoor" in a way likely to cause confusion about the origin of the project.

If you are unsure whether a specific use falls inside or outside fair use, ask. We would rather have a quick conversation than discover a problem later.

## Requesting permission

Send a written request to:

[frontdoor@buildbandits.com](mailto:frontdoor@buildbandits.com)

Include:

- A description of the intended use.
- The audience and the scope (geographic, commercial, time-bound).
- Mockups or examples where relevant.

We aim to respond within 14 days. Permission, when granted, is non-exclusive and may be revoked if the terms of use change materially.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the project matures or as the trademark is formally registered. Material changes will be announced in the CHANGELOG and in the release notes.
